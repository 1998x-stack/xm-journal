# Journal article scorecard (100 points)

| Dimension | Weight | Release expectation |
|---|---:|---|
| Fidelity to the user's narration | 25 | No invented facts; feelings, uncertainty and plans remain distinct |
| Narrative clarity | 20 | One main thread; context and concrete details before abstraction |
| Visual explanation | 20 | Original SVGs clarify real relationships and fit the article |
| Personal usefulness | 15 | Insights lead to realistic, user-grounded next actions |
| HTML, accessibility and responsive design | 10 | Semantic structure, keyboard access, mobile and print layouts |
| Validation and browser QA | 10 | Local assets, no overflow, no console errors, meaningful interaction |

Release gate: >=90 and no critical factual, privacy, accessibility or rendering defects. The score is an editorial review aid, not a measured metric. Never invent a score to claim quality.
